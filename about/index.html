OUR STORY

Built on 30 Years of Real-World Production Delivery

Powerhouse PNW is a small and efficient software development and DevOps team operating out of the tech corridor of Bellevue, Washington.

Most of our journey has been spent in the trenches writing and releasing software across a 30-year span for massive tech companies—including long tenures delivering core systems for industry titans Microsoft and Oracle. We have seen software lifecycles evolve from bare-metal on-premises servers and bespoke rack infrastructure to distributed multi-cloud architectures.

Over the last 4 years, our focus has expanded intensely into modern DevOps: transforming fragile release cycles into automated, self-healing CI/CD pipelines, container orchestration, and declarative cloud infrastructure.
